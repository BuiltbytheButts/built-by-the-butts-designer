This is the Diamond End Grain Designer image used as the visual target for the sample build. Keep it available throughout the process, then compare it with the two finished-board photos in Step 10.

1

Original build photos

Mill the lumber

Mill the selected lumber square and straight, allowing extra material for planing and sanding.

2

Original build photos

Rip and arrange the laminate strips

Rip the strips oversize, finish them consistently, and dry-fit the species in the planned order before glue is applied.

3

Original build photos

Glue and flatten the laminated blank

Clamp the strip assembly evenly, then flatten and mill the blank square to the required size before the 45° cuts.

4

Original build photos

Complete the first four 45° cuts

Mark the centers of adjacent edges and make the four 45° cuts before beginning the Edge Rip operation.

5

Original build photos

Cut the Edge Rip

After the first 45° cuts are finished, cut across the walnut section. The opposing angled shoulders are visible on the left and right sides of this end view.

6

Original build photos

Glue the new 45° maple onto the cut walnut edges

Fit the maple replacement pieces to the freshly cut walnut edges. Keep the mirrored assemblies aligned so the light lines meet cleanly when the pattern is assembled.

7

Original build photos

Assemble the long master blank and glue borders before crosscutting

Complete the long patterned blank first. Check its assembled width, then glue the border stock to the two long edges before any crosscuts are made.

8

Original build photo

Crosscut the master blank and keep every piece in order

Cut perpendicular to the long strips using the spacing and kerf calculated for the project. Keep every crosscut in sequence so the pieces can be rotated correctly during the next dry fit.

9

Original build photo

Rotate and lay out the crosscuts

Keep the crosscuts in order. Rotate every second piece and dry-fit the diamond field before the final glue-up.

10

Finished build photos

Glue, flatten, trim, sand, and finish

Glue the final field, flatten both faces, trim to size, sand through the selected grits, and apply the chosen finish. Compare the completed board with the original Designer reference shown at the beginning of this guide.
